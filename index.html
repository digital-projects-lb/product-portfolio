Product Lifecycle Simulation Game

An interactive 10-stage delivery simulator built to help junior product managers, business analysts, and stakeholders practice RACI governance and match the right framework artifacts from concept to launch.

Play Live Simulation ➔ Read Product Docs

1. The Problem

New product managers and cross-functional team members often struggle to remember who owns decisions at each delivery stage and which governance documents are needed before moving forward.

Static slide decks and dense PDF playbooks rarely stick because people learn governance best by making decisions hands-on.

2. The Solution

I designed and built a browser-based simulation that turns the 10-stage product lifecycle into a guided, hands-on workflow.

Players unlock each milestone by aligning stakeholder RACI roles and matching the required framework deliverable before advancing.

3. Product Decisions & How It Was Built

  • 10-Stage Progressive Highway: Structured the flow from Concept and Discovery through Tech Engagement, Sprints, UAT, Launch, and Post-Launch Expansion so learners see end-to-end dependencies.
  • Active Gatekeeping Logic: Built validation rules that block stage advancement until both stakeholder ownership (RACI) and deliverable matching are verified.
  • First-Time User Onboarding: Added a 4-step "How to Play" tour and contextual clues so new players understand the rules within 30 seconds without external instructions.
  • Browser Persistence: Used local storage so users can pause mid-lifecycle and resume their progress anytime without needing a login.
