Product Path Game

A browser-based simulation that navigates digital product managers and cross-functional teams through the 10-stage product lifecycle path.

Designed and built a 10-stage interactive web resource for Digital Product Managers and cross-functional teams to align on decision ownership and deliverables, complete with exportable working templates and guided user onboarding.

Play Live Simulation ➔ Read Product Docs

The Problem

Product management sits at the intersection of Business viability, Technology feasibility, and the Customer value proposition.

The Solution

I designed and built Product Path, a browser-based simulation that turns the 10-stage product lifecycle into a guided, hands-on workflow.

Players unlock each stage along the road by assigning team RACI roles (Responsible, Accountable, Consulted, Informed) and matching required deliverables to the respective stage to unlock working document templates and the next product stage.

Product Decisions & How It Was Built

  • 10-Stage Product Road: Mapped the end-to-end journey from Concept through Launch.
  • Step-by-Step Checks: Built rules to unlock document templates once team RACI roles and stage deliverables match.
  • Quick Onboarding: Added a short "How to Play" tour so anyone can jump straight in.
  • Browser Storage: Enabled automatic progress saving so users can pick up where they left off without needing a login.
