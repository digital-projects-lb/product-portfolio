Product Documentation

Product Documentation Suite

Practical product management documents defining the vision, business case, functional requirements, and technical setup behind the Product Path simulation game.

1. Business Case

Overview Summary

Aligning Digital Product Managers, Business Analysts, and delivery teams on decision ownership and stage deliverables leading into frameworks such as BABOK (Business Analysis Body of Knowledge), PMBOK (Project Management Body of Knowledge), RACI (Roles and Responsibility), and Agile Scrum, emulating the Product management function of providing team cohesion to deliver profitable outcomes.

Objectives & Value

  • Clear Decision Ownership: Help teams confirm Responsible (R), Accountable (A), Consulted (C), and Informed (I) roles across 10 standard delivery stages.
  • Zero-Cost Delivery: Built as a lightweight static web app requiring no backend servers, databases, or user logins.
  • Practical Working Templates: Provide ready-to-use stage templates and one-click PDF exports that teams can reference day-to-day.

2. Business Requirements Document (BRD)

Core Functional Requirements

  • Sequential Stage Progression: Users progress through stages 1 to 10 in order, with a clear prompt if they click a locked future stage early.
  • RACI Role Check: Each stage validates 7 stakeholder roles (Product, SLT, Sponsor, UX/CX, Dev, Legal, Risk) against expected ownership.
  • Deliverable Matching: Users drag and drop the matching deliverable pill for the active stage before unlocking the document template.
  • Template Preview & PDF Export: Once roles and deliverables match, the stage's working template unlocks with one-click PDF printing.
  • Guided Onboarding Tour: First-time visitors get a short 4-step walkthrough modal, with an option to reopen it anytime from the header.

3. Business Analysis Summary

Business Needs

  • Digital Product Managers & Delivery Teams: Using Product Path as a practical reference to confirm role ownership and required documents at each delivery stage.
  • Product Leaders: Reviewing the application to evaluate structured product thinking, workflow clarity, and hands-on execution.

Workflow Analysis

Product Path breaks the workflow into 10 focused stages with Kanban sticky-note clues that show clearly why ownership shifts between Product, Engineering, Legal, and Sponsors at each milestone—and which documents to produce at each stage.

Product Path highlights the importance of each stage and stakeholder group, ensuring early buy-in, input, and sign-off from the right people. It presents Digital Product Management as a unified path from initial concept and market analysis—gaining Senior Leadership support and demonstrating profitability and market-share growth—through to cross-functional delivery and business value.

4. Technical Brief

Architecture Overview

  • Frontend Stack: Built with React and Vite for fast local development and lightweight static bundle generation.
  • Data-Driven Phase Engine: All 10 stages, RACI rules, hints, and artifact templates are decoupled into a single configuration object (metrics.js), making content updates possible without touching UI logic.
  • Client-Side State Persistence: Uses browser localStorage to save active stage, unlocked progress, and tour completion state with zero backend overhead.
  • Hosting: Deployed as static assets via GitHub Pages for high availability and zero ongoing hosting costs.
