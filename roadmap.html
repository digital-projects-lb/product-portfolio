Project Scope & Delivery Plan

Project Scope & Gantt Timeline

Clear scope boundaries and an 8-week delivery schedule showing how Product Path progressed from initial concept through interactive build, usability refinements, and portfolio launch.

In Scope (v1.0 & v1.1 Release)

  • 10-Stage Product Road: Interactive SVG progress map from Concept through Post-Launch.
  • Team Roles Matrix (RACI): Live validation across 7 core product and delivery stakeholders per stage.
  • Deliverable Matching: Drag-and-drop pills linking practical product deliverables to each stage.
  • Working Templates & PDF Export: Fill-in-the-blank product templates with one-click styled PDF printouts.
  • Guided Tour & Auto-Save: First-time user walkthrough and browser storage so players can resume anytime.

Out of Scope (Planned for v2.0)

  • User Accounts & Cloud Sync: Excluded in v1 to keep access instant with zero login friction.
  • Custom User-Editable Inputs: Allowing teams to type directly into templates before printing is slated for v2.0.
  • Vertical Split-Screen Layout: A side-by-side vertical road view is planned for the next release.
  • Team Leaderboards: Deferred to keep v1 focused on self-paced learning and day-to-day reference.
